# Business Roadmap

See [../BUSINESS_PLATFORM/BUSINESS_PLATFORM_ROADMAP.md](../BUSINESS_PLATFORM/BUSINESS_PLATFORM_ROADMAP.md)
for the detailed phase plan.

Summary:

| Wave | Modules | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Decision + skeleton | Written first `bNN` |
| 1 | Finance or inventory spine | One ledger balances |
| 2 | Sales + purchase | Documents post |
| 3 | Tax + treasury + warehouse | Registers and payments |
| 4 | Manufacturing and downstream | Inventory truth exists |
| 5 | Industry packs | Two horizontal documents SoR-Live |

Nothing in this wave plan is implemented as `business/bNN_*` today.
