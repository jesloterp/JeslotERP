# Funding and Talent

**Owner:** Chetan Patel  
**Status:** Intent — not a claim that a round has closed or that seats are hired.

JeslotERP will not be built by hope. It will be built by **capital** and
**paid specialists** on a locked sequence. This page is for investors and
for developers who want product-level work.

## What an investor is buying

Not a replacement for any third-party ERP on day one. Not a fork of any
existing open or licensed suite.

| Asset | What it is |
|---|---|
| Kernel | 33-package multi-tenant ERP OS (SoR-Live slice; Production withheld) |
| Architecture | This public repository — reviewable without an NDA theatre |
| Requirements | Finished `b01`–`b17` specs so build does not rediscover scope |
| Plan | [NINETY_DAY_PLAN.md](NINETY_DAY_PLAN.md) — books, then stock, then two posting invoices |
| Aim | [FOUNDER_AIM.md](FOUNDER_AIM.md) — next-level ERP, record time, open source only if good |

Risk is honest: business ledgers are **not implemented**. The bet is that a
real kernel plus finished specs plus paid owners beats another app-first
clone.

## Use of funds (intended)

| Bucket | Why |
|---|---|

| Paid platform owner | Keep p01–p33 stable; metadata/number/process spine |

Funds are **not** for a marketing site that pretends the suite exists, for

No valuation, raise amount, or instrument is published here. Those belong in
a private conversation with Chetan Patel after the investor has read this
tree.

## What a paid developer is selling

Time on the **critical path**. Evidence first:

- A module or ledger you have shipped (finance, inventory, workflow, IAM, metadata)
- Comfort with PostgreSQL RLS, hexagonal Python, and fail-closed APIs
- Willingness to follow a requirement spec instead of inventing a product

| Role | Owns | Joins when |
|---|---|---|
| Platform engineer | Spine: metadata, numbers, process, RLS | Day 1 |
| Finance engineer | `b01` journals and period | Day 1 |
| Inventory engineer | `b07` movements | After trial balance |
| Tax / commercial engineer | `b03` + one AR + one AP | After stock truth |
| Security reviewer | Threat sign-off | Before any Production label |

Rates and contracts are private. This repository does not crowd-source
unpaid “equity for a dream.”

## Why speed needs money

Record time is elapsed calendar. Calendar collapses when:

- two owners are paid to sit on finance and the spine at the same time
- requirements are already written (they are)
- kernel is not redesigned (it must not be)

Without funds, the same sequence still applies — it just loses the record.
Chetan Patel is pursuing the market so the record stays possible.

## Why open source is later

Open source is a **quality and trust** move, not a hiring hack.

- Documentation is Apache-2.0 **now** so the architecture can be judged.
- Source stays private until books post, threat review is signed, and the
  product is good enough that opening it helps the ecosystem instead of
  dumping a half-suite.

See [ROADMAP/OPEN_SOURCE_ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP/OPEN_SOURCE_ROADMAP.md).
