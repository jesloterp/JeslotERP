# Founder Aim — Chetan Patel

JeslotERP exists because the world already has application-first ERPs
(products such as **ERPNext** and **Odoo**) and licensed enterprise suites
(products such as those from **SAP** and **Microsoft**). What it does not
have is a modern Python platform that is as serious as the latter and as
reachable as the former.

Those names identify **other companies’ products**. JeslotERP is independent
and unaffiliated. See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

That is the entire aim.

## The sentence

Build the **operating system for ERP** first. Then put real books, stock, tax,
and commercial documents on that OS. Do it in **record-winning time**. Pay
people who can own a module. Raise funds so the critical path is staffed.
Open the source **only when the product is good enough to deserve it**.

Anything that does not serve that sentence is out of scope.

## Why this is not a clone

Application-first ERPs won by shipping many documents quickly. Their gravity
is the application. JeslotERP’s gravity is the **kernel**: identity,
organization, metadata, numbers, process, rules, audit, sharing, ALM, output,
integration.

Licensed enterprise suites won by making posting, period, org, and transport
non-negotiable. JeslotERP takes that **discipline** (not those products) and
refuses a closed-stack price of entry.

A clone of either camp is a waste of Chetan Patel’s time and of any
developer’s career. The product must be **next level**: platform-first,
posting-honest, metadata-driven, multi-tenant, fail-closed.

## What already exists (so we do not start from zero)

- Thirty-three platform packages specified and, in the private kernel,
  SoR-Live for the shipped HTTP slice.
- Partner and company metadata pilots.
- Finished requirement specifications for `b01`–`b17`.
- Public architecture so a serious person can judge the work in a day.

What does **not** exist yet: posted journals, a stock ledger, a tax engine,
or an open-source implementation. Those are the next wins — not another
platform number.

## What winning looks like

| Horizon | Win |
|---|---|
| Record sprint | A company can post a balanced journal, lock a period, reverse a document, and read a trial balance — on the existing kernel |
| Next sprint | Items move; on-hand equals the sum of movements |
| Commercial sprint | One AR invoice and one AP invoice **post** (tax calculated, not typed into a PDF) |
| Fundable story | Architecture public, first ledger live, paid owners named, use of funds obvious |
| Open source | Only after the product is good: honest kernel, real ledger, signed threat review |

Winning is **not** seventeen dimmed Desk tiles. Winning is **not** a press
release that says Production. Winning is a date on a posted document that
survives audit.
