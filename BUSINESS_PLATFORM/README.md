# Business Platform

The business platform is the ERP application layer. It is numbered separately
from the kernel:

- Kernel: `p01_identity` … `p33_sharing`
- Business: `b01_finance` … `b17_service`

**Documentation:** requirement specifications for all seventeen modules are
**finished** under [REQUIREMENTS/](REQUIREMENTS/README.md).

**Code:** **No** `business/bNN_*` implementation was found. Desk tiles are not
ledgers.

Product goal (Chetan Patel): **enterprise posting** on the kernel — an
independent product, not a clone of any third-party ERP. See
[../MARKET_POSITION.md](../MARKET_POSITION.md) and [../TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md).
